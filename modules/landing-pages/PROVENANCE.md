The initial open-source edition adapted the user-supplied “Universal Landing Page Design Guidelines (ULPG), version 0.3 — Proposed correction release.” Version 0.4.0 added entry patterns, discovery/navigation, returning journeys, demonstrations, brand/editorial storytelling and freshness. Preserve the ULPG contributors' MIT notice in LICENSE. External standards and linked material retain their own ownership; references do not imply endorsement or certification.

Integration revision 1 relocates the twenty complete sections without changing their wording. The standalone preamble, application workflows and release footer are replaced by GUIDE.md's DDM routing and scope contract. Source examples and templates are retained as illustrations/scaffolds. The historical references.md describes the standalone edition's optional companions; in this package DDM is the host method, while Web HIG remains optional and independent. No external standards were refreshed as part of migration.

Section numbers remain upstream identifiers. Individual migration IDs encode section and line position and are locally assigned because upstream did not provide individual normative IDs. traceability.json records every source section and each line containing a requirement keyword, plus all seven prohibitions governed by section 16's MUST NOT. Section hashes preserve surrounding context, applicability and recommendation strength as well as the identified lines. The matrix is audit evidence, not a second rule authority.

No implementation, user evaluation or behavioral baseline/candidate comparison is claimed. Retiring the independent source repository requires separate authorization and completed preservation evidence.
