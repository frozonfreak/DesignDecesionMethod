# Illustrative v0.3 / v0.4 scenario comparison

**Fictional briefs; heuristic documentation review only.** The comparison below was performed by reading the repository's v0.3.0 guidance and candidate v0.4.0 wording against the same brief in each row. No pages were built, public websites audited, user sessions observed, interactions tested or performance measurements taken. These are scoped judgments about guidance coverage, not empirical validation of a design or the specification.

## Method and scope

For each brief, compare v0.3 sections 3, 4.2, 6–9, 11, 14–16 and 18 with their v0.4 counterparts, including new sections 4.5, 4.6, 6.1, 8.3, 9.1, 11.1 and 15.4. Ask whether a suitable entry and discovery experience can be justified without imposing a hero template, transaction funnel, decorative technique or unsupported evidence. Check that the candidate retains accessibility, necessary decision information, truthful claims and project-specific performance requirements.

The v0.3 baseline already permits varied page types through sections 4.1 and 14. Its section 4.2 nevertheless requires the “essential proposition” to be understandable and describes a common commercial hero. The candidate replaces that narrow phrase with subject/offering/task and adds an explicit selection model; this comparison does not claim v0.3 prohibited alternative layouts.

## Limits and follow-up

The documentation review found an explicit rationale for each proposed experience in v0.4 while retaining the listed safeguards in the wording. This does not establish that designers will produce better pages or that an implementation will preserve those safeguards. All five implemented-design outcomes remain **unverified**; validation method here is **heuristically reviewed** for guidance coverage only.

To evaluate designs, build or inspect baseline and candidate pages from each unchanged brief. Use representative first-time and returning visitors, supported mobile/desktop and input contexts, relevant find/explore/resume tasks, accessibility checks, and demo fallback/error checks where applicable. Compare comprehension and task outcomes using project-defined criteria. Record field and laboratory evidence separately. Use the [review template](../templates/review.md) to record actual methods, results, limitations and responsible reviewers; none of that follow-up testing is claimed here.
