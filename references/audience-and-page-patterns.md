# Audience and page-pattern prompts

Read when marketing communication needs domain-specific visitor questions or evidence prompts. These method-authored examples are optional and nonexclusive; they are not industry templates or assumptions about demographic groups. Use actual roles, locale, language, device, connectivity and the brief to choose relevant questions. Skip this reference for unrelated work.

| Context | Questions to investigate | Evidence to seek | Common pitfalls |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agricultural SaaS | Can field staff record daily work on their actual devices? What happens with poor connectivity? Which reports follow from entries? | Real connected workflows, device constraints, and verified offline or recovery behaviour | Stock-field imagery replacing product evidence; inventing offline support |
| B2B SaaS | Does this fit my role, integrations, budget and approval process? What must each audience evaluate before committing? | True product screens, supported integrations, security limits, pricing and implementation conditions | Unsupported ROI or implementation promises; forcing different roles through one journey |
| Ecommerce | Does the product fit? Which variations exist? What is the total delivered price, delivery uncertainty and return policy? | Accurate product images, terms, availability and known versus unknown costs | Concealed costs, fabricated reviews or delivery guarantees |
| Professional services | What expertise and scope are offered? Which work supports the claim? How do I make a relevant enquiry? | Verified case work, capabilities, credentials and contact routes | Generic jargon, inflated credentials and invented client outcomes |
| Editorial or portfolio | What is the story or body of work? How can I explore it at a useful depth? | Authored work, meaningful media and reading or discovery routes | Forced signup, transactional CTAs or operational dashboard conventions |

Combine prompts when roles overlap. Keep high-consequence prerequisites and limitations at the decision point regardless of domain. Retain the chosen brand and visual system; these examples do not prescribe colour, layout, motion or section counts. Missing evidence should become an explicit unknown or a labelled proposal, not a fabricated artifact or product claim.
